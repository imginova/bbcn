      How to play
      
    
      FNAF backwards. You control the animatronics. The night guard is played by the computer. Catch it before 6 AM (a night lasts 3 minutes).
      
    
        
      
      • Pick an animatronic: click its card, press 1-9 (0 for the tenth), or Tab through them.
        • 
      
      • Walk: click a lit room next to it on the map. Each one needs a few seconds between moves.
        • 
      
      • Attack: from the W. Corner, the E. Corner or Vent C, click LEFT, RIGHT or VENT on the office square (or press E). If the guard doesn't stop it in time, you win.
        • 
      
      • Abilities: every animatronic has one (press Q or its button). Some never attack at all: they only help.
        • 
    
      
    
      The guard only knows what it sees and hears. The red REC on the map is the camera it's watching: a room it's watching sees you. Every move might be heard. When it notices an attack it needs a moment to react: attack while it's busy on the cameras, winding the music box, rebooting or frozen.
      
    
      The office cam shows the doors, the vent, the guard's power and what it's doing. A shut door costs it power: keep it scared, and when its power runs out, you win.
      
    
      Being stopped sends that animatronic home, frozen for a few seconds.
      
    
      P or Esc pauses, M mutes. The night pauses by itself if you switch windows.
