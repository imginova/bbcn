HOW TO PLAY

    
The run

    
    
      
  • A run is several nights in a row. Before each night you're shown 5 cards. Take as many as you like (or none): they join your lineup for the rest of the run, and everyone in your lineup is out there every night.
    • 
      
  • Each character has a rarity: Common 5, Uncommon 10, Rare 20, Epic 35 and Legendary 50 points. Rarer ones are harder and turn up less often.
    • 
      
  • Survive a night with a lineup worth 150 points or more and you win the run.
    • 
      
  • You have 3 lives. Getting caught (or passing out from the heat) costs one, and you replay that night with the same lineup. Lose all three and the run is over.
    • 
    

    
The office

    
    
      
  • Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM. Each hour lasts 30 real seconds.
    • 
      
  • The monitor, each closed door, the flashlight and the fan each use the same amount of power. The mask and the audio lures are free. If the power runs out, everything shuts off and Freddy finds you in the dark.
    • 
      
  • The office heats up all night. Switch the fan on to cool it down; at 100% you pass out.
    • 
      
  • The flashlight lights up the area around your mouse pointer (hold F or the right mouse button). It works in the office, in the doorways (only while that door is open) and on the cameras.
    • 
      
  • Keep an eye on the two windows next to the doors: some things only show up there.
    • 
      
  • On nights with someone it works on, the controlled shock (X, or the Shock button) zaps the camera you're looking at. Each shock costs a little power.
    • 
      
  • The monitor has a CAMS tab and, when something is in the vents, a VENTS tab. On VENTS, click a vent to play an audio lure there: anyone in a vent next to it may follow it and stay to listen.
    •
