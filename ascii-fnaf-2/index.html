    HOW TO PLAY
    
    
      
      
    • Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM. Each hour lasts 30 real seconds.
      • 
      
    • Power: the monitor, each closed door and the vent door use power, a heavy door twice as much, and every shock a little. The mask is free. If the power runs out, everything opens and Nightmare Freddy finds you in the dark.
      • 
      
    • Oxygen never comes back. Minireenas, the Nightmarionette, Ennard in the vents, Jack-o Chica's smoke and Plushtrap all use it up. At 0% you pass out.
      • 
      
    • You can't see anyone in the doorways. Go to a door and listen (Q or E): you can still work that door's buttons there, but not the cameras, the mask or the shock.
      • 
      
    • The shock: at the desk it zaps everything in your office; on the monitor it zaps the camera you're looking at.
      • 
      
    • Nightmare Bonnie (left hall, CAM 1), Nightmare Chica (right hall, CAM 2), Nightmare Fredbear (either hall) and Nightmare Foxy (either side, never on camera, listen for his steps) come to the doors. Shut the heavy door, and only open it once the breathing has stopped.
      • 
      
    • Nightmare Freddy: Freddles climb onto your desk. Shock the desk before there are 4.
      • 
      
    • Circus Baby beeps softly down the left hall. When she's in, put the mask on until she's right in front of you, then shock the desk.
      • 
      
    • Ballora: her music comes from the side she's heading to, louder each step. Shut that door.
      • 
      
    • Funtime Freddy: his script is on the office wall. At an important line (marked !), watch the auditorium (CAM 4): the side of the room he walks to is the door he's going to. Shut it.
      • 
      
    • Funtime Foxy: be watching the show stage (CAM 3) at the time on the SHOWTIME sign.
      • 
      
    • Ennard either walks down a hall to a door (shut it) or crawls into the air vents (shock the desk).
      • 
      
    • Minireenas and the Nightmarionette appear in your office and use up oxygen. One shock zaps all the Minireenas; the Nightmarionette needs 3.
      • 
      
    • Withered Bonnie walks in: mask on until he leaves.
      • 
      
    • Bidybab squeaks in the vent above you. Count: on every 3rd squeak, close the vent door.
      • 
      
    • Jack-o Chica stands in a doorway breathing smoke (you can see it drift in). Slam the heavy door on her.
      • 
      
    • Plushtrap gets into the oxygen room (CAM 5). Stay on his camera until he leaves.
      • 
      
    • Nightmare Cupcake eats power in the power room (CAM 6, a pink ! on the map): shock that camera. Nightmare Mangle makes three cameras blink ~ but is only on one: find them and shock it. A wrong shock wastes power.
