HOW TO PLAY

    
    
      
  • Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM. Each hour lasts 30 real seconds.
    • 
      
  • Three shapes, ◆ ▲ ●, walk from the stage toward your office. ◆ always comes to the left door, ▲ to the right, and ● picks a side.
    • 
      
  • You can hear the shapes move. Each one has its own note, and its steps get louder and lean toward its side the closer it gets.
    • 
      
  • When ◆ or ▲ steps into a doorway you see it there and hear it trying to get in: a thud, a rattling handle and a creak. You have about three seconds to shut that door. If the door is shut, the shape goes back to the stage.
    • 
      
  • ● never shows up in the doorway. When it arrives you hear a loud rumble from its side instead, with the same three seconds to react. If both doors are shut when it arrives, it waits outside; the moment you open either door it rumbles again and goes for that one.
    • 
      
  • ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°) sleeps in the music box on CAM 7. The box slowly unwinds; hold W (or the Wind button) while watching CAM 7 to wind it back up. When it gets low, the Camera button warns you. If it runs out, he comes for you and nothing can stop him.
    • 
      
  • ■ creeps up to either door in three steps: far, medium, close. One more step and it gets you, and doors don't stop it. You hear a loud scrape when it gets close. Hold a door light (Q or E) to see it; keep holding and it gets pushed further back. A shut door blocks the light. From far away it may sneak over to the other door.
    • 
      
  • (: hides in your office. If you hear a giggle after lowering the camera, find (: somewhere in the office and click it quickly.
    • 
      
  • ★ Modifiers (ticked on the custom night screen) give each threat a twist: ◆ Charger freezes while you watch it but rushes when you don't; ▲ Trickster fakes its door sound (the real one is always in the doorway); ● Patient waits growling behind a shut door; Lenny's Wanderer box moves between cameras 2, 3, 4 and 7; ■ Bold ignores the light once close (shut that door for 2 seconds instead); (: Twins hides two at once.
    • 
      
  • Watch the hallway cameras (5 and 6) for early warning, then close the door before the shape arrives.
    • 
      
  • Doors, door lights and the camera use power. If power hits 0%, everything shuts off and you are on your own.
