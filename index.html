HOW TO PLAY

    
    
      
  • Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM. Each hour lasts 30 real seconds.
    • 
      
  • Three shapes, ◆ ▲ ●, walk from the stage toward your office. ◆ always comes to the left door, ▲ to the right, and ● picks a side.
    • 
      
  • You can hear the shapes move. Each one has its own note, and its steps get louder and lean toward its side the closer it gets.
    • 
      
  • When ◆ or ▲ steps into a doorway you see it there and hear it trying to get in: a thud, a rattling handle and a creak. You have about three seconds to shut that door. If the door is shut, the shape knocks and goes back to the stage.
    • 
      
  • ● never shows up in the doorway. When it arrives you hear a loud rumble from its side instead, with the same three seconds to react. If both doors are shut when it arrives, it waits outside; the moment you open either door it rumbles again and goes for that one.
    • 
      
  • ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°) sleeps in the music box on CAM 7. The box slowly unwinds; hold W (or the Wind button) while watching CAM 7 to wind it back up. When it gets low, the Camera button warns you. If it runs out, he comes for you and nothing can stop him.
    • 
      
  • ■ creeps up to either door in three steps: far, medium, close. One more step and it gets you, and doors don't stop it. You hear a loud scrape when it gets close. Hold a door light (Q or E) to see it; keep holding and it gets pushed further back. A shut door blocks the light. From far away it may sneak over to the other door.
    • 
      
  • (: hides in your office. If you hear a giggle (often right after lowering the camera, but it can wake up on its own too), find (: somewhere in the office and click it quickly.
    • 
      
  • Ж crawls from CAM 7 across the Stage and the Dining hall, then down either the east side (Supply, East hall) or the west side (Backstage, West hall) to your office. Click it on the camera it's on to push it back one room, then follow it and keep clicking until it's home on CAM 7. If it reaches your office, click it there quickly.
    • 
      
  • ¤ buzzes at one of your doors. Flash that door's light for a moment and it jumps to the other door; flash it there too and it leaves. Doors don't stop it, and a shut door blocks the light.
    • 
      
  • Ψ appears right in your office. Put the mask on (W, or the Mask button) and keep it on until it leaves. The mask covers your view except for two eye holes, and you can't use the cameras with it on, but doors, lights and clicking through the eye holes all still work.
    • 
      
  • Ξ and § crawl through the vents toward your office. Watch them on the monitor's VENTS tab (V) and click a vent room to play an audio lure there (lures need no power, but take a moment to recharge). Ξ goes to a lure up to two rooms away from it (counting through the vents). § only follows a lure in a room next to it: a lure in its own room startles it one vent closer! A loud bang means one has reached V6, right next to you: you have a few seconds to lure it away before its next move gets you.
    • 
      
  • ¥ can peek in at any time, even while the monitor is up: listen for a sharp ting. Drop the monitor and put the mask on; about a second is enough to send it away.
    • 
      
  • © pops up ads on your monitor while it's up. Each ad covers the whole monitor, whatever tab you're on: you can't see or use anything on it (cameras, winding, lures, tasks) until you close it with its [X]. If four are open at once, the system crashes and it gets you.
    • 
      
  • Modes (picked on the custom night screen): Standard is the normal night. In Generator you start with 0% power: hold G (or the Generator button) to charge, but while you hold it both doors fly open and you can't do anything else. Nothing moves for the first 30 seconds, so charge up first, and everything costs half as much power. At 0% everything just switches off until you charge again. In Tasks you win by finishing every task on the monitor's TASKS tab (T): hold the mouse button on a task (or hold its number key) and it fills while you hold; letting go, lowering the monitor, switching tabs or an ad pauses it.
    • 
      
  • ★ Modifiers (ticked on the custom night screen) give each threat a twist: ◆ Charger freezes while you watch it but rushes when you don't; ▲ Trickster fakes its door sound while you're on the cameras (the real one is always in the doorway); ● Patient, when shut out, waits behind the door; when it growls (after about 2 seconds), open the door, let it attack once more and slam it again (open too soon and it just waits again); Lenny's Wanderer box moves between cameras 2, 3, 4 and 7; ■ Flinch lunges a step closer (up to close) if you let go of the light before it's gone; (: Twins hides two at once; Ж Stubborn takes two clicks per room; ¤ Ping-pong needs a third flash; Ψ Double take sometimes fakes leaving (the fake exit sound rises at the end) and comes right back; Ξ Wise ignores a lure in the room it just followed one to; § Quick moves 1.5 times as often while you're not looking at the VENTS tab; ¥ Double always comes back once more right after leaving; © Sneaky can put an ad's [X] in any corner.
    • 
      
  • Watch the hallway cameras (5 and 6) for early warning, then close the door before the shape arrives.
    • 
      
  • Doors, door lights and the camera use power. If power hits 0%, everything shuts off and you are on your own.
