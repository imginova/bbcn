LINE 1

Two doors, a flashlight, a mask, a vent full of noises and a battery that never lasts. Seventeen of them are out there tonight, and every one of them plays by its own rules.

Turn sound on: most of them warn you with a sound first.

An unofficial fan game. Not affiliated with Scott Cawthon or Five Nights at Freddy's.

HOW TO PLAY

    
    
      
  • Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM. Each hour lasts 30 real seconds.
    • 
      
  • The monitor, each closed door and the flashlight each use the same amount of power. The mask and the audio lures are free. If the power runs out, everything shuts off and Freddy finds you in the dark.
    • 
      
  • The flashlight lights up the area around your mouse pointer (hold F or the right mouse button). It works in the office and on the cameras.
    • 
      
  • The monitor has a CAMS tab and, when something is in the vents, a VENTS tab. On VENTS, click a vent to play an audio lure there: anyone in that vent or the one next to it may follow it and stay to listen.
    • 
      
  • Freddy walks from the stage through the dining area and a hall to either door. Each move he laughs from the side he's heading to, louder as he gets closer, and you can follow him on the cameras. You won't see him in the doorway: on the third laugh, shut that door.
    • 
      
  • Bonnie comes to the left door and Chica to the right one. Watch them on the cameras and shut the door when they're in the doorway.
    • 
      
  • Freddy, Bonnie and Chica stay on the stage (CAM 1) all night when they're switched off.
    • 
      
  • Foxy gets ready in Pirate Cove (CAM 3). Shine the flashlight on the cove to send him back. If he runs, shut the left door; his bang costs some power.
    • 
      
  • Golden Freddy can be in your office when you flip the monitor down. Tap the mask on, quickly.
    • 
      
  • Toy Freddy walks in while the monitor is down. Put the mask on and keep it on until he leaves.
    • 
      
  • Toy Bonnie (fast, easily lured), Toy Chica (medium, hard to lure) and BB (never on the vent map) crawl through the vents. When one climbs in, keep the mask on until it makes a sound and leaves. If BB gets you, he takes your flashlight for 20 seconds.
    • 
      
  • Springtrap is slow in the vents and easy to lure, but the mask doesn't fool him: never let him climb in. A loud bang means someone is in the last vent.
    • 
      
  • Freddy Endo wanders the vents and never attacks, but you can't play a lure in his vent.
    • 
      
  • Mangle crawls across your ceiling. Flash them 3 times (take the light off them in between) before they reach the other side.
    • 
      
  • The Puppet waits in the music box on CAM 7. One click of Wind (or W) fills it. A ♪! on the Camera button means it's getting low.
    • 
      
  • A Toy cupcake can pop up anywhere, even over the monitor, blinking and beeping. Tap it in time.
    • 
      
  • Shadow Freddy sits on a random camera; its map button blinks a red !. Find him and shine the light on him.
    • 
      
  • Shadow Bonnie turns the office dark. Search with the flashlight for his eyes (never over the vent) and hold the light on them.
    • 
      
  • The Crying Child is on the camera the sticky note in your office names. Don't watch that camera too long. He never moves to the camera you last put the monitor down on.
    •
